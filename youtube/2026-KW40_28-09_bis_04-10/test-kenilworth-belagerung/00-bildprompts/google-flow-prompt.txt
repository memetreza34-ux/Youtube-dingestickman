GOOGLE FLOW MASTER PROMPT — Warum Hunger eine Burg besiegen konnte

Create 18 separate 16:9 historical explainer illustrations for one coherent YouTube video.

CHANNEL STYLE:
Use one consistent hand-drawn 2D historical explainer style across the full set. Human characters, when needed, belong to the same expressive historical stickman family: simple round or slightly oval warm off-white heads, slim simplified bodies, clean dark ink outlines, flat muted historical colors, subtle cel shading and a light handmade ink-and-paper texture. Characters may differ through historically appropriate hair, beards, eyebrows, helmets, hoods, clothing, armor, tools and body language. Faces stay simple but expressive.

Non-character images such as castles, maps, objects, supply systems and overviews must use the exact same line, color, shading and texture language. Mature historical documentary/explainer feeling, not childish or goofy. Historical plausibility is more important than decorative fantasy.

Do not use photorealism, 3D, Pixar, anime, realistic portrait faces, painterly realism, graphic-novel realism, modern corporate infographic style, pseudo-text, watermarks or image numbers inside the images. Default: no visible text.

IMPORTANT GENERATION RULES:
- Bild 01 is cover + first scene. Generate exactly 3 temporary candidates for Bild 01, choose the strongest one, keep only the winner as `Bild 01.png`.
- Bild 02 through Bild 18: generate exactly one accepted final image each.
- Work in batches of maximum 5 active generations.
- All accepted images must look like the same illustrator made them for the same channel.
- Characters are NOT required in every image.
