GOOGLE FLOW MASTER PROMPT — Warum Hunger eine Burg besiegen konnte

Create 18 separate 16:9 historical explainer illustrations for ONE coherent YouTube video.

CHANNEL STYLE:
Use one consistent hand-drawn 2D historical explainer style. Clean dark ink outlines, flat muted historical colors, subtle cel shading, light handmade ink-and-paper texture. Human characters use the same expressive historical stickman family: simple warm off-white round/oval heads, slim simplified bodies, simple readable faces, historically appropriate hair, beards, hoods, helmets, clothing and props. Mature and cinematic, not childish.

Every image must have ONE dominant subject and no more than 1–3 supporting elements. Keep important forms large and easy to read on YouTube. Do NOT create wimmelbilder, museum infographics, textbook posters, encyclopedia plates, dense crowds, tiny central subjects, dozens of mini-figures, many labels, arrows everywhere, collages, multi-panel layouts, unnecessary cutaways or excessive micro-detail.

No photorealism, 3D, Pixar, anime, realistic portrait faces, painterly realism, graphic-novel realism, fantasy armor, modern objects, logo, watermark or image numbers.

VISIBLE TEXT:
None by default. Do not invent English labels or pseudo-text. If any visible text is ever explicitly requested, it must be short, exact and German.

VIDEO CONTINUITY LOCK:
This entire video takes place around the same visual version of Kenilworth Castle in 1266. Exterior continuity: pale tan-gray medieval stone, massive central keep, strong curtain walls, broad gatehouse, surrounding water/moat landscape and the same overall castle silhouette in every exterior view. Interior continuity: dark stone walls, heavy timber shelves, rough wooden barrels and grain sacks. When the storage cellar returns, reuse the SAME room layout and nearly the SAME camera angle so depletion is obvious. Recurring defenders keep the same clothing, hair, beards and colors when shown again. Do not redesign the castle, gate, cellar or recurring people from scene to scene.

GENERATION RULES:
- Bild 01 is cover + first scene. Generate exactly 3 candidates, choose 1 winner, keep only `Bild 01.png`.
- Bild 02–18: exactly one accepted final image each.
- Maximum 5 active generations at once.
- All accepted images must look like the same illustrator created the same historical world.
