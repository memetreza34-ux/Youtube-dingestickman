CHANNEL STYLE — USE ONCE PER FLOW BATCH
Use one consistent hand-drawn 2D historical explainer style across all images in the batch.

When people are needed, use expressive historical stickman characters from the same character family: simple round or slightly oval warm off-white heads, slim simplified bodies, clean dark ink outlines, flat muted historical colors, subtle cel shading and a light handmade ink-and-paper texture.

Characters may differ through historically appropriate hair, beards, moustaches, eyebrows, helmets, crowns, hats, hoods, head coverings, clothing, armor, accessories, tools and body language. Faces stay simple but emotions must be readable. Characters must feel alive and individual, not like blank identical mannequins.

When people are NOT needed, use maps, architecture, landscapes, objects, systems, symbolic scenes or historical overviews in the exact same line, color, shading and texture language.

Mature historical documentary/explainer feeling. Not childish, not goofy.

Do not use photorealism, realistic portrait faces, 3D rendering, Pixar style, anime, painterly realism, graphic-novel realism, modern corporate infographic style, image numbers, pseudo-text or watermarks.

Default: no visible text inside generated images.
