VISUAL-FORM FIDELITY — MANDATORY:
- comparison: both comparison poles must actually be visible and clearly opposed.
- cause-effect: cause and consequence must be visually connected.
- process-sequence: the changing state must be immediately readable, preferably through continuity of place/angle.
- system-hierarchy: hierarchy must be expressed through space, height, routes or relationships, not corporate arrows.
- battle-city-overview: spatial situation remains the main idea, not a random close character shot.
