VISUAL DECISION RULE
Before writing each image prompt:
1. Identify the spoken idea.
2. Choose the best visual function.
3. Choose the best visual form.
4. Reduce it to ONE clear visual statement.
5. Choose ONE dominant subject.
6. Add no more than 1–3 necessary supporting elements.
7. Define camera/composition.
8. Apply continuity lock for recurring places/figures/rooms/objects.
9. Only then write the prompt.

Supported forms include:
- character scene
- environment only
- map / geography
- object focus
- architecture / city
- system / hierarchy
- cause → effect
- process / sequence
- comparison
- symbolic metaphor
- battle / city overview
- rise / fall lifecycle

Do NOT force stickman characters into every image.

CHANNEL STYLE — USE ONCE PER FLOW BATCH
Use one consistent hand-drawn 2D historical explainer style across all images in the batch.

When people are needed, use expressive historical stickman characters from the same character family: simple round or slightly oval warm off-white heads, slim simplified bodies, clean dark ink outlines, flat muted historical colors, subtle cel shading and a light handmade ink-and-paper texture.

Characters may differ through historically appropriate hair, beards, moustaches, eyebrows, helmets, crowns, hats, hoods, head coverings, clothing, armor, accessories, tools and body language. Faces stay simple but emotions must be readable. Characters must feel alive and individual, not like blank identical mannequins.

When people are NOT needed, use maps, architecture, landscapes, objects, systems, symbolic scenes or historical overviews in the exact same line, color, shading and texture language.

Mature historical documentary/explainer feeling. Not childish, not goofy.

Avoid: overcrowded scene, busy crowd, wimmelbild, museum infographic, textbook poster, encyclopedia plate, tiny central subject, dozens of small figures, many labels, arrows everywhere, pseudo-text, English labels, fake handwriting, collage, multi-panel layout, split-screen border, unnecessary cutaway, excessive micro-detail, photorealism, realistic portrait, 3D render, Pixar, anime, painterly realism, graphic-novel realism, fantasy armor, modern objects, logo, watermark, image number.

Default visible text: NONE.
If short text is absolutely required, it must be explicitly requested, exact, brief and German.
